## Benchmarks and implementation findings
- Proposed root cause for non-deterministic GLM-5.3-Flash-TP2 recall: A16 MoE decode sums eight experts per token with unordered BF16 atomics, FP32 summing runs only in prefill; sparse attention keeps only 2,048 of 516K tokens, amplifying tiny noise; dense split-K BF16 atomics add more. Marked as unconfirmed code reading. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542496172176973936/1559486636537155687)
- On 2x RTX PRO 6000 Max-Q with fp8 branch and A4 prefill, the same atomic settings made cached decode byte-identical but locked in shifted-twin quantities 191 and 219 instead of the true records; cold prefill still varied and record 002600 remained missed (341). No big speed difference in limited testing. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542496172176973936/1559533666877837422)
- On the fp8 branch (karmic-kraken-beta-20261011.2, glm53-tp2 preset, 2x RTX PRO 6000 Max-Q at 300 W, temp 0), record 005700 identical/nonce went from 0/8 and 7/8 before to 8/8 and 8/8 after; 003700 still flipped. With A16 prefill, replacing shared experts and attention weights with BF16 gave 8/8 on both records, and A16 vs A4 differences were observable; fits TP2 with CSF and 1M context. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542496172176973936/1559509745357037599) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542496172176973936/1559537925408952332) [(3)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542496172176973936/1559538278196187137)
- Same-host A/B at 516K context on 2x PRO 6000 at 300 W: reasoning_effort=max fixed recall on SGLang v0.6.1 plus ormandj checkpoint (24/24, 8/8) and vLLM 20261011.2 plus main A16 DCP2 MTP3 (15/15, 8/8). GSM8K identical; prefill tied; vLLM decode 4-18% slower; SGLang per-request high did not override max server default. Production fp8/A4 at max also passed 8/8 and 10/10, reported as a near-tie pass, not a fix. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542496172176973936/1559565835033514005) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542496172176973936/1559627887978610763)
