## Benchmarks and implementation findings
- Qwen3.8-Flash-Next on SM120 with 32GiB VRAM (2x16) plus system RAM was reported stable, with aider eval tests added; it was still running after 3 days of abuse. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1528331644933767190/1559978782272659667) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1528331644933767190/1559987367522074675)
- vLLM logs MTP speculative-decoding warnings for Qwen3.8-Flash-Next: no KV cache group identified as the draft model's, and num_speculative_tokens > 1 may lower acceptance rate. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1528331644933767190/1559847648977231993)
- On 4x RTX 3090, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next prefill throughput was 5.08-5.38k tok/s for 2048-token batches with CUDA graph disabled. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1528331644933767190/1559987553841446933)
- On an RTX 6000 PRO, Ling-3.1-flash Q4 with CPU offload to DDR5 at about 225 GB/s measured 16 tok/s decode and 135 tok/s prefill. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1466898004219789502/1560047302230544476)
- slopllm runs only at DCP8 with about 4M context on 8 cards and has host cache built in. On a CSF checkpoint it measured about 8% lower prefill than vLLM and 20-30% higher decode than DCP8, without i8 ring transport. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542910591730192434/1559962887122985010) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542910591730192434/1560102698836168786)
- With GLM-5.3-Flash, switching from high to max reasoning can increase output tokens and response time by up to 3x; the reporter chooses SGLang over vLLM for practical latency. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1542496172176973936/1559959749523673179)
- In DGX Station, no P2P/ReBAR; BAR is set to 256MB; prefill may go through host unless NCCL is explicitly directed not to do that. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1516097906967576706/1559945352294179020) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1516097906967576706/1559946045155315874) [(3)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1516097906967576706/1559946437146443948)
- vLLM logged GPU KV cache size 2,296,678 tokens and maximum concurrency 2.19x for 1,048,576-token requests. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1517199740444475423/1560153556256952401)
- GLM-5.3-Flash W4A16 NVFP4 K32 Experts-FP8 WO under SGLang was reported at 46.2% pass_rate_1 and 89.3% pass_rate_2 on Aider over 225 cases with zero error outputs. [(jump)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1471527895439638528/1560003409828905090)
