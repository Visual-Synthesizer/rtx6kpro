## Regressions and user reports
- A user reported their RTX PRO 6000 host caps the GPU PCIe link at Gen4. Another user said multi-GPU units run PCIe5 while the single-GPU layout lacks a redriver, and Gen5 can be set via KVM. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1562385096386813973) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1562389855143596075) [(3)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1562390545358258220)
- Engram's heavy random 4K lookups are reported to trigger controller-internal read-reclaim/background media writes on a Lexar NM790 (MAP1602); same image/config dropped from ~1000 to ~200 tok/s at CC32 with IOPS falling after 1-2s, linked to known MAP1602 behavior. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1562516217539989566) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1562516929564909599) [(3)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1547563567379906651/1562518493767667794)
- Reported on Flare: ~21-37s of single-core CPU work before the first token on long conversations, with GPU and disk idle. The same conversation on vLLM (20261020) showed no such delay, and /props returned 404 to OWUI. Resuming a 608-message/537K-token agent session took 240s of preprocessing. Switching to a different Flare build removed the ~30s wait but lost --embedding-table-resident-scales. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1562576166005510165) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1562598920482197525) [(3)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1562674519917994036)
- Flare's --prefix-cache-disk was described as unreliable compared with vLLM's LMCache disk cache. In a reported reproduction, an identical 130K-token prompt cached 130031/130031, while changing one trailing token gave cached=0/130031 and a ~9.1s full re-prefill; vLLM's block-hash cache reused all but the last block in that case. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1562638653967044639) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1562651701477904406)

OWUI collisions were traced to duplicate sub-tool names; renaming colliding tools fixes them. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1562718860551913546) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1562565421985701951)

## Benchmarks and implementation findings
- Flare was reported stable while riding at the VRAM limit (95.522Gi/95.593Gi), unlike vLLM; on an RTX 6000, 97200MiB was reported without crashing. With DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash TP4, --memory-limit=-8G was reported to reserve about 32GB for another model while keeping ~11.5M context tokens, similar to vLLM. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1562517135870402642) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1517199740444475423/1562480071229309029) [(3)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1562604606582693919)
- Reported: Flare loads DeepSeek in under 30s. On a 4-Spark DeepSeek run, the lead rank holds the full weights while the other three hold ~1/4 of the total package, with Flare distributing shards itself. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1562449385382486099) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1562449999336308758)
- On dual Spark with Qwen FN TP2 on Flare, reported prefill was 2,081-2,594 tok/s (TP1, from disk with 8GB RAM rows) compared with 3,097-3,381 tok/s (TP2, from RAM); TP2 scaled better in concurrency. Another user reported Qwen FN TP2 working much better and said this explained why chain had not been production. [(1)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1560667258232115220/1562427052290998314) [(2)](https://discord.com/channels/1466898002793857221/1517199740444475423/1562426371341418587)
